What's in the rack

Only one Ethernet run comes in from the router, so the UniFi switch fans that out to wired connections for everything else. Beszel monitors both stargazer and mirage colloid. Every unit has a custom 3D-printed rack mount — except the MeLE, which just sits in its bay for now.

U6 Philips Hue Bridge — Smart lighting: Hue lights, zones, and automations. Printables
U5 UniFi USW-Flex-2.5G-5 — Fans the single router uplink out to wired runs for the rest of the rack. TBD
U1–U4 TerraMaster F4-424 Pro (i3-N305, 32GB DDR5) + 4× Seagate BarraCuda Pro 12TB stargazer Primary NAS — runs ~90% of my Docker containers, Plex, and Frigate. MakerWorld
